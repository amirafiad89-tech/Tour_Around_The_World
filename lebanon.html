Lebanon

Lebanon is a charming country located on the Mediterranean Sea. It combines green mountains, the blue sea, and a rich history. It is known for its rich culture, delicious cuisine, and world-famous hospitality

Lebanese Culture

Lebanese culture is a rich blend of traditions, history, and modern lifestyle. Lebanon is known for its diversity, where different religions and cultures live together in harmony. This diversity makes Lebanese society unique and vibrant. Hospitality is one of the most important values in Lebanon. People are friendly, welcoming, and love to share food and moments with others. Lebanese cuisine is famous worldwide for its delicious dishes like tabbouleh, hummus, and kibbeh. Music, art, and celebrations are essential parts of daily life. Lebanon also combines modern lifestyle with deep historical roots, making it one of the most culturally rich countries in the Middle East.

Currency of Lebanon

The official currency of Lebanon is the Lebanese Pound (LBP). It is also known as the Lebanese Lira. Although the local currency is widely used, the US Dollar is also commonly accepted in many places.
