About Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is one of the most important countries in the Middle East. It is known for its rich history, Islamic heritage, and vast deserts. Riyadh is the capital, while Mecca and Medina are the holiest cities in Islam.

Saudi Culture

Saudi culture is deeply rooted in Islamic traditions and Arab customs. It is known for hospitality, traditional clothing, and strong family values. Major celebrations include Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, along with cultural festivals and events.

Famous Places

Medina: Home of the Prophet’s Mosque.
