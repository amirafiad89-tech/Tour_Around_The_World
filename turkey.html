About Turkey

Turkey is a beautiful country located between Europe and Asia. It has a rich history that includes many civilizations such as the Ottoman and Byzantine empires. Turkey is famous for its unique culture, stunning landscapes, and important role in tourism.

Turkish Culture

Turkish culture is a mix of Eastern and Western traditions. People are known for their hospitality and kindness. Turkey is famous for traditional markets like the Grand Bazaar, delicious cuisine, and cultural celebrations and festivals.

Famous Places

Hagia Sophia: A historic mosque and former church, one of the most famous landmarks in Istanbul.

Blue Mosque: A stunning mosque known for its blue tiles and beautiful architecture.

Cappadocia: Famous for its unique rock formations and hot air balloons.

Topkapi Palace: The residence of Ottoman sultans for centuries.

Grand Bazaar: One of the oldest and largest markets in the world.

Pamukkale: Natural white terraces formed by mineral-rich waters.
